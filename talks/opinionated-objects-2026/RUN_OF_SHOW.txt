ONE-SENTENCE ANSWERS
- “Isn’t this just Cloudflare Radar?”
  Cloudflare supplies one standards input; Lens adds observed bot behavior and an independent content-recovery result, with all three exposed separately.

- “Why average them?”
  Equal thirds make the opinion explicit; a missing source leaves the aggregate unfinished instead of changing the weights.

- “Does Readability provide that score?”
  No. Readability provides the extraction; Lens publishes four transparent binary checks over its output.

- “Why Readability and not something else?”
  It is Firefox Reader View’s engine, so it is the extractor most readers have
  actually met. We ran it against Defuddle over five pages before switching: on
  /garage/horizon, Defuddle handed over 14 of 26 control labels as prose and
  Readability hands over 3. It is not uniformly better, which is why the control
  count stays on screen: on stripe.com it leaks 5 where Defuddle leaked 2.

- “Is a 200 response the same as agent access?”
  No. Retrieval, interpretation, permission, and action are separate; Lens keeps those distinctions visible.
