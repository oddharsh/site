Tooltip Experiments

Variants tried for the photo grid and tracklist on aadhar.sh. Hover the slots and rows. The two photo variants below (Fuji LCD and XP-skinned camera readout) carry the same content shape, with different chrome; the third (vinyl) is a track-specific candidate. Status pills: in review are live candidates, shipped made it to the homepage, rejected got tried and didn't, reference is a comparison point.

The live candidates share one architecture: popover API for show/hide, @starting-style for the entry fade, a small rAF-coalesced translate3d loop for cursor tracking. Roughly 100 lines of JS total.

Fuji X-T LCD readout (photos) in review

Hover any slot. Floating panel mimics the camera's image-review screen: histogram band on top, filename, exposure trio, recipe grid, footer. Follows the cursor. The slot itself stays untouched (no in-tooltip thumbnail; the larger slot image is already visible).

Recipe values are real EXIF, pulled from /images/metadata.json. Earlier preview had fabricated values (XT508756 was tagged ACROS+R, which is B&W only); fixed.

XP-skinned camera readout (photos) in review

Same content as the Fuji LCD above (histogram, exposure trio, recipe grid, date) but reskinned in XP visual vocabulary: cream panel, blue gradient title strip, sunken bevel around the histogram, Tahoma body with Courier numerics. Keeps the camera-back density without arguing visually with the surrounding XP shell.

Vinyl sleeve at cursor (tracks) in review

Hover any row. A square album cover with a paper-mat border and a slight -6° tilt floats at the cursor, like pulling a sleeve out to read the front. Same architecture as the photo tooltip; the row text stays put.

Polaroid flip (photos) rejected

Rejected: hover flips the slot 180° via transform: rotateY, which means you see the metadata or the photo, never both. The whole point of the grid is the photos, so a tooltip should add to what's visible, not replace it. The same fault killed the first Fuji-LCD overlay design (v1, painted on top of the slot). Kept here as a record.
